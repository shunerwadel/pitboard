### Topology

* *Suspension topology slide*  
* **Suspension topology** \- the carefully arranged mechanical layout of linkages and force elements (springs and dampers) which connect our unsprung and sprung masses.  
  * Design and ability to adjust varies greatly depending on the series in which we are racing  
  * Front and rear topology will be at least slightly different (front has steering), and can be vastly different  
* Review common examples across the top forms of motorsports  
  * Example: F1 (team designs everything)  
  * Example: IndyCar (spec series)  
  * Example: NASCAR Cup series (spec series)
