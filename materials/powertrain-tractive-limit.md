# Tractive force limit

Longitudinal acceleration is limited by the lesser of **power**, **gearing**, and **tire μ · Fz** at the drive axle.

## Useful bounds

Power-limited acceleration (idealized):

```
ax ≈ P / (m · v)
```

Tire-limited:

```
ax ≤ (μ · Fz_drive) / m
```

Drive-axle normal load rises under acceleration (longitudinal transfer), which helps traction — until the tire saturates or the engine cannot deliver torque through the selected gear.

## Classroom cue

Plot tractive force vs speed with engine torque × gear ratio overlayed on the tire friction circle budget. Students see why first gear lights up the tires and top gear feels soft.
