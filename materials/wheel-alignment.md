### Wheel alignment

* *Wheel alignment slide*  
    * **Camber** \- Angle between a tilted wheel plane and the vertical (Z) axis  
      * Unit of degrees (SI unit of radians)  
    * **Toe** \- Direction the wheel plane points relative to the longitudinal (X) axis  
    * **Caster**\- Angle between steering axis (straight line between outer upper and lower control arm pivots) and the lateral (Y) axis.   
      * Causes a self centering effect since the contact patch is behind the pivot.
