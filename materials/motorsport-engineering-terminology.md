### Motorsport engineering terminology

* *Motorsports terminology slide*  
* There are terms in motorsports which describe performance or characteristics of any pair of tires and/or associated suspension  
  * Front / rear axle \- refers to front or rear pair   
    * Abbreviated as F and R  
    * Does not refer to a mechanical axle on the car  
  * Left / right side \- refers to left or right pair  
    * Abbreviated as LS or L, and RS or R  
* The term “corner” is commonly used for an individual tire and/or associated suspension  
  * Abbreviated as FL, FR, RL, RR or LF, RF, LR, RR  
* Vertical movement (**heave**)  
  * Car’s vertical movement relative to the ground is called heave  
    * Slang terms: travel or bump/droop
