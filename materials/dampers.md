### Dampers

* *Dampers slide*  
* **Damper (shock)**\- hydraulic mechanical device designed to dissipate kinetic energy from suspension movement by converting it into thermal energy (heat)  
* Controls how fast the suspension moves  
  * Mechanics  
  * As the damper is compressed or extended (rebound), a piston inside the body moves through a chamber filled with hydraulic oil  
  * The oil is forced through tiny, restrictive valves  
  * The faster you try to move the piston, the harder it pushes back (same principle as aerodynamics we studied before).  
  * *Pass around physical damper*  
* The configuration and size of the values through which the oil flows is highly adjustable  
  * Shim stacks on a piston \- internal change that requires rebuilding  
  * Clicks on the body \-  external setting easily adjusted  
  * Adjustments can be made to in both or a single direction  
* Characterization  
* *Review damper curves*  
  * Dampers are measured by force shaft velocity curves / table  
    * Low-speed behavior controls low frequency movement caused (body roll during corner steering, pitch during braking, etc).  
    * High-speed behavior controls high frequency movement caused by the track surface (hitting a sharp curb, etc).
