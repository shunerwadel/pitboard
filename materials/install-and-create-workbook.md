# Week 4: The race weekend

* Test your course knowledge by competing against fellow students in mock race weekend sessions \- run simulations, analyze results, test your changes, get driver feedback, and develop the fastest setup.  
* Recap of setup development  
  * Many conceptual sims long time before race event to determine overall design  
    * For F1, design of next year’s car  
    * For IndyCar/NASCAR, to inform what regulations for upcoming seasons  
  * Many performance based sims in weeks leading up to event  
  * One at a time fine tuning by race engineers, week of  
    * This is where we are focusing today  
* How are race teams set up?  
  * F1, NASCAR, Indycar  
* Sprint race challenge  
  * Each get 10 mins to drive  
    * Top 3 on 3 different teams  
    * Go over driving techniques and data  
  * Assign teams  
    * One driver, one engineer  
  * Give baseline setup  
  * Let them setup the sim tool based on the stats of the car  
  * Give them a “things to consider” slide  
  * 30 mins before practice give them a notebook of information  
    * Will have last year’s setup and notes on what could be better  
  * Let them generate a run plan or changes they want to try  
  * 30 min practice session, use time anyway they want  
    * Long runs, short runs  
  * Post-Practice Debrief  
    * Recap session (plan going in, did we stick to it)  
    * Changes \- review with driver comments talking through each one  
    * Talk about why we decided to make the changes we did  
  * Final tune, engineer drives (5 mins)  
    * Final tweaks  
    * Simulates back at the shop DiL testing  
  * Race (15 laps)  
    * Best 10 lap average wins (can throw out one lap)  
    * 10 sec bonus if you complete all laps with no incident (at my discretion)  
  * Post-Race Debrief  
  * Class conclusion  
    * Recap main points from each class
