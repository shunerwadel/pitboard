### Coordinate systems

* *Coordinate systems slide*  
* **Cartesian coordinate system**  
  * Method which allows for locating points in space  
  * Uses an origin (0, 0, 0\) and 3 axis system (with signed directions)  
  * Motorsports engineering nomenclature  
    * X axis \| longitudinal (fore/aft) \| roll (rotation about)  
    * Y axis \| lateral (left/right) \| pitch (rotation about)  
    * Z axis \| vertical (up and down) \| yaw (rotation about)  
* Coordinate systems allow us to know:  
  * Where points of interest are relative to each other on the car (chassis frame)  
  * Height of certain points relative to the ground (ground frame)  
  * Where the car or ground is relative to anything else (global frame)  
* Course mostly focuses on chassis and ground frame
