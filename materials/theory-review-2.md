### Theory review

* *Performance metrics slide*  
* From learnings so far:  
  * Adding load to tires gives us more lateral capability  
  * Transferring load across tires gives us less lateral capability  
  * We want as much downforce (CL) as we can get for as little penalty in drag (CD) \- maximize L/D  
  * We need a ride characteristics the driver can handle  
  * We want to accelerate as quickly as possible (big engine, big brakes)
