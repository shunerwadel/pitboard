### Setup Management

* How do we arrive at the setup we want to put on the car?  
      * Historical information and simulation (will cover in week 2\)  
    * Communication  
      * During event \- physical setup sheet, chat message  
      * Short term \- report in an email  
      * Long term \- database  
    * Measurement  
      * *Measurement slide*  
      * Need to know what setup is actually achieved by mechanics  
      * Individual parts measured and stored in a database  
        * Parts can have their own “local” coordinate systems  
      * **Coordinate measuring machine** (CMM) \- Romer, Faro, Leica  
        * Measures suspension pivots, camber, toe, heights  
      * Scales and data acquisition system (DAQ)  
        * Measures weight distribution, component loads, heights
