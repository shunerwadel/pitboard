### Mechanical linkages

* *Suspension linkage slide*  
* Solid mechanical linkages and components which connect the force elements and sprung to unsprung suspension  
  * Examples  
    * Uprights, wishbones or control arms, rockers, push rods  
  * Design considerations  
    * Achieve specific kinematic behaviors based on placement of pivot or attachment points (camber curves, etc)  
    * Limit compliance   
    * Weight and packaging  
    * Aerodynamics  
  * Often there is some adjustability in the inboard/outboard mounting position of the mechanical linkages  
    * Trade off for adjustability is weight and packaging
