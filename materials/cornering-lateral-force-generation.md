### Cornering (lateral) force generation

* *Lateral tire force slide*  
* How are lateral forces generated?  
  * The driver steers the wheel  
  * The tire does not instantly follow the new direction; its tread and carcass deform laterally  
  * **Slip angle** \- angular difference between the direction a tire is pointing and the actual direction it is traveling  
  * Tread develops shear forces against the pavement  
  * The summed shear forces act sideways on the tire, producing lateral force (Fy) that allows the car to corner
