### Scope of this course

* *Scope of course \- car types slide*  
    * There are many types of race cars  
      * Dirt sprint cars, stock cars, offroad trucks, formula cars  
    * *Scope of course \- circuit types slide*  
    * Review kinds of circuits (or tracks)  
      * Street courses, road courses, ovals  
    * This course focuses on race cars designed to operate on paved surfaces (both oval and road courses), though what we cover will generally apply to all forms of auto racing
