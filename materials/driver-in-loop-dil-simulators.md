### Driver in loop (DiL) simulators

* *DiL slide*  
    * **Drive in loop (DiL) simulator** \- real-time vehicle dynamics simulation platform that integrates a human driver into the control loop of a virtual vehicle  
      * Continuous exchange of physical inputs from driver and feedback from vehicle model in real time  
      * Allows us to learn about and improve our simulation toolchain by adding the driver “in the loop”  
    * Use cases  
      * Driver training for new track or newer drivers  
      * Engineering tool that allows us to close the gap between the various simulation results and the driver’s on track experience  
    * DiL systems  
      * Cockpit  
        * Made as realistic as possible  
        * Same brake system, steering, seat/insert, etc  
        * Steering motor attached at end of column to generate feedback  
      * Graphics  
        * Gathered by same companies that scan tracks  
        * Projected onto screen or LED wall  
      * Platforms (sDiL vs motion DiL)  
        * Static or motion capable  
        * Motion platforms can move in heave, yaw, translational  
        * Multiple styles of platform made by a few different companies  
          * ABD, Accusim, Dynisma  
      * Cueing \- process of transforming outputs from the vehicle model into sensory signals meant to convince the driver's brain that they are experiencing real-world vehicle motion  
        * Cueing settings vary driver to driver based on physical differences and preference  
        * Examples of cueing settings: rates and amount of platform movement, timing between graphics and platform movement  
        * Very difficult to get right where drivers feel comfortable in DiL for multiple hours  
        * Breakdown on establishing good cue settings causes driver frustration with the DiL
