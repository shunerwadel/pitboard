### Other general concepts

* Track Mu \- Scalar used to adjust the global grip level between the track and in simulations  
  * Grips level evolve frequently over short time periods at track so useful to have simulation knob to match  
* Lab experiments \- virtual simulation experiments also exist to replicate lab testing  
  * Pull down / Push up rig  
  * K\&C  
  * Shaker rig (7-post)  
* Batch / DOE \- ability to send many simulations to be run simultaneously  
  * Base setup and variations  
  * Multiple base setups and variations
