### Drive (longitudinal) force generation

* *Lateral tire force slide*  
* How are longitudinal forces generated?  
  * A driving torque is applied to the tire by the vehicle’s drivetrain  
  * When the tire starts spinning slightly faster than the vehicle is traveling, longitudinal shear forces are generated at the contact patch  
  * **Slip ratio** \- proportional difference between the rotational speed of the tire and forward speed of the car  
  * For a given tire and vehicle setup, there is a slip ratio which produces the most longitudinal force
