### Fundamental learnings

* *Fundamental aero learnings slide*  
    * Aerodynamic behavior is highly non-linear and hard to fully understand  
    * Aero forces scale quadratically with speed  
    * We want as much downforce (CL) as we can get for as little penalty in drag (CD) \- maximize L/D  
    * Aero force contribution to balance changes drastically with speed \- more contribution at high speeds  
    * Aero forces are the main source of vertical load on the car  
    * We need to understand our car's aerodynamic behavior so we can optimize our setup (aero platform)
