### Setup Definition

* *Setup sheet slide*  
* **Setup** \- Specific, documented configuration of a vehicle's settings, components, & measurements optimized for a given event, track, & driver.  
  * Manifestation of all engineering efforts, translating simulations & data into operational instructions for mechanics.  
  * Historical record to benchmark performance, track & vehicle evolution, & replicate baselines in future sessions.
