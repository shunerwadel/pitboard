### Basic balance theory

* *Basic balance theory slide*  
* Covering at a high level here will re-visit in more detail later  
* Types of balance  
  * **Understeer** (tight) – front tires slipping (less lateral capability) more than rears \- stable but can be slow  
  * **Oversteer** (loose) – rear tires slipping more than fronts \- less stable but potentially faster  
  * Neutral \- neither understeer or understeer  
* Race engineer wants to know about balance from the driver ASAP once the car is on track, and will ask frequently if balance has changed  
* Need this information to start thinking about the next changes  
* Bottoming and “ride” are other common questions  
* Common for driver to use number scale (1 to 10\) and organize feedback by corner segment  
* Mechanical setup and aerodynamic properties of the car have biggest effect on balance
