### Metrics

* **Lateral load transfer distribution (LLTD)** \- the axle based distribution of lateral load transferred from the inside to outside tires  
      * Generally expressed as a percent of distribution at the front axle  
      * Mechanical balance metric \- we can adjust with suspension  
      * Higher value \= more weight transfer at front \= more understeer  
    * **Aero balance** \- distribution of aero forces between front and rear of a car and their subsequent impact on the car’s balance  
      * Generally expressed as percent of aero load at the front axle  
      * Balance metric \- we can adjust with wing or body changes  
      * Higher value \= more aero load at front \= more
