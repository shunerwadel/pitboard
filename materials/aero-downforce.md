# Downforce and drag

Wings and underbodies increase normal load on the tires without adding mass — but they cost drag.

## Efficiency

Aero efficiency is often discussed as:

```
L/D = Downforce / Drag
```

Setup is rarely “max downforce.” It is the best **lap-time** compromise for that track: high-speed corners reward downforce; long straights punish drag.

## Balance

- Front wing / splitter primarily loads the front axle.  
- Rear wing / diffuser primarily loads the rear.  
- Changing ride height and rake changes underbody performance and can shift aero balance with speed.

## Classroom cue

“At 120 km/h, how much extra Fz does 200 N of downforce buy compared to ballast?”  
Same normal load, **zero** extra sprung mass — that is why aero wins when the rules allow it.
