### Intro

* *Aerodynamics intro slide*  
* Why are tires and aero first order concerns when it comes to designing or setting up a race car?   
  * Because in the most general sense, we are moving a solid body through a fluid while (generally) in contact with a solid surface  
  * In engineering, we need to initially think about problems at a very basic level.   
* We could answer, “because we are moving a car on a paved surface through air”, but describing it more generally allows us to consider problems we will face without pre-conceived restrictions  
* **Aerodynamics** \- the study of how air moves and the forces it exerts on solid objects moving through it  
* The better we understand how the air flows over, under, and around our particular racecar, the more we can optimize it for performance  
* **Aero platform** \- We can learn how to position our car relative to the air and track surface to optimize aerodynamic performance (similar to tires)  
* Aero forces are the largest source of vertical load for our tires and the biggest restriction on how fast we can go in a straight-line (drag)
