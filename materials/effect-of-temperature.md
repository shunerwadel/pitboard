### Effect of temperature

* *Effect of tire temp slide*  
* Temperature (temp)  
  * Tires are designed to operate optimally in a certain temp range  
  * Temp greatly impacts molecular adhesion between track and contact patch  
    * A hot tire off a race car is literally sticky to the touch  
  * Changing temp results in charging internal pressure and thus behavior  
    * Will typically start tire pressures low so they build to desired operating pressure  
  * Brakes are a major source of heat build in a tire
