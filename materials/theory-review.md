### Theory review

* *Balance review slide*  
* **Understeer** (tight) – front tires slipping (less lateral capability) more than rears \- stable but can be slow  
* **Oversteer** (loose) – rear tires slipping more than fronts \- less stable but potentially faster  
* **Neutral steer** (neutral) \- neither understeer or understeer  
* On track our driver will tell us the balance as they perceive it  
  * Varies depending on driver  
* In simulation, a basic way to look at balance is comparing the tire slip angles between front and rear  
  * More slip in the front is understeer (tight)  
  * More slip in the rear is oversteer (loose)  
* Overtime, we gain a feel for what sim predicted balance means on track  
* We typically want the car just to the side of understeer  
  * Would rather err more stable given uncontrolled variables (weather)  
  * For a high HP car, we need to protect rear tires for corner exit
