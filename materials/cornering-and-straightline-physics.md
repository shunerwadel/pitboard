### Cornering and straightline physics

* *Cornering and straightline physics slide*  
* Will use free body diagrams to help us understand straightline and cornering forces  
* **Center of gravity** (CofG)  
  * Exact virtual balance point of the car's mass in all three dimensions   
  * Theoretical point where the entire vehicle’s weight is concentrated and through which all external gravitational and inertial forces act  
  * Lower is better; limits sprung body movement and reduces lateral load transfer (more on this later)  
* Vertical forces  
  * Weight and aerodynamic downforce balanced by track normal forces  
* Lateral forces  
  * Centrifugal force balanced by tire cornering forces  
* Longitudinal forces  
  * Aerodynamic drag, tire rolling resistance, and inertial force balanced by thrust (from power unit) and braking force  
* If forces in the same plane on either side of the CofG are not balanced we create a moment about the CofG  
* Constant speed (or stopped) if sum of forces in any direction is equal  
* Accelerating if forces are not equal
