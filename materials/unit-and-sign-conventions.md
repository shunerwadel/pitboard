### Unit and sign conventions

* Must use a common convention to effectively utilize a coordinate system and communicate about any object with features (like a race car)  
* *Units and sign conventions slide*  
* Two main conventions: SAE vs ISO  
  * **SAE** \- Society of Automotive Engineers  
  * **ISO** \- International Organization for Standardization  
  * Differences  
    * \+Z, SAE is down, ISO is up  
    * \+Y, SAE is driver’s right, ISO is driver’s left  
  * Similarities  
    * \+X is forward  
    * Both follow right hand rule  
* Racing series may use SAE, ISO, or their own convention
