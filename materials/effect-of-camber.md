### Effect of camber

* *Camber contact patch slide*  
* Why do we want negative camber on a road course, and negative camber on the outside tires with positive camber on the inside tires on an oval?  
  * Larger contact patch during cornering  
  * **Camber thrust** \- force generated from the tire due to the tire leaning into the corner creating additional shear force  
* Why can’t we run large camber all the time?  
  * Accelerates wear (ride only on the inside rim of the tire down the straights) \- might run higher amounts for shorter events (like qualifying)  
  * Reduces the contact patch during straight-line acceleration \- typically run lower camber angles in the rear since most race cars are rear wheel drive
